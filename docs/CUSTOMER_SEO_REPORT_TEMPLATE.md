# Customer SEO Report Template

> **Usage:** This template is designed for agency account managers or SEO specialists to export SeoSuite findings into a client-friendly format.

---

## 1. Executive Summary
*Provide a 2-3 sentence overview of the current state of the page. Highlight the most significant win and the most urgent blocker.*
**Example:** The newly published "Spring Collection" landing page has excellent foundational structure and fast load times. However, it currently lacks semantic keyword coverage and has critical indexability issues that will prevent it from appearing in search results.

## 2. Overall SEO Score
**SeoSuite Score:** `[XX]/100`  
**Score Band:** `[Excellent / Good / Needs Improvement / Poor / Critical]`

## 3. Critical Blockers
*List any severity="critical" issues that actively harm the site or prevent indexing.*
- **[Issue 1]:** *e.g., Robots meta tag is set to 'noindex'. Search engines cannot read this page.*
- **[Issue 2]:** *e.g., Target keyword is completely absent from the title and introduction.*

## 4. Quick Wins
*List 3-5 severity="high" or "medium" issues with low estimated effort.*
- **[Win 1]:** *e.g., Add the target keyword to the H1 heading.*
- **[Win 2]:** *e.g., Include a meta description (currently missing).*
- **[Win 3]:** *e.g., Break up the 600-word block of text with at least two H2 subheadings.*

## 5. Semantic & Content Opportunities
*Summarize findings from the Semantic analysis and NeuronWriter enrichment (if available).*
- **Topic Coverage:** `[Good / Missing Core Entities]`
- **Suggested Terms to Add:** `[Term 1], [Term 2], [Term 3]`
- **Content Depth:** *e.g., The competitor average for this topic is 1,200 words, but your article is currently 400 words. Consider expanding the 'How to Install' section.*

## 6. AI Visibility Readiness
*Overview of how well the content is structured for LLMs (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews).*
- **Readiness Level:** `[High / Medium / Low]`
- **Key Enhancements Needed:** *e.g., The page lacks structured Schema.org markup and has no clear Q&A formatting. Adding a FAQ block will dramatically improve the chances of AI citation.*

## 7. Internal Linking Recommendations
*Suggestions for passing link equity.*
- **Link out to:** *e.g., Your main category page ("Summer Apparel").*
- **Get links from:** *e.g., Your recent blog post about "Top 10 Fashion Trends".*

## 8. Next 7-Day Action Plan
*Provide a concise checklist for the client's content team.*
1. [ ] Remove the `noindex` tag.
2. [ ] Rewrite the H1 to include the primary keyword.
3. [ ] Add a 50-word FAQ section at the bottom of the page.

### Disclaimer
**No Ranking Guarantee:** Search engine algorithms (Google, Bing) are complex, proprietary, and constantly evolving. While this report provides heuristic best practices based on industry standards, implementing these recommendations does **not** guarantee specific search engine rankings, traffic increases, or inclusion in AI Overviews (LLM citations). This report is a structural and semantic guide, not a warranty of performance.
